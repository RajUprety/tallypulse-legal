Privacy policy.

Ultimate Counter · Effective July 16, 2026

At a glance

Ultimate Counter needs no account, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking SDK. Your counter records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Data the app handles

The app stores the names, values, history, goals, categories, notes, reset rules, reminders, widget choices, and appearance settings you create. Depending on your counters, this may include personal health or lifestyle information. The developer does not receive this data.

Storage and security

Active app data is kept in Android's private app storage and widget storage, protected by the operating system. No counter record is sent to a developer server. After an exported file leaves the app, its protection depends on the location or service you choose.

Permissions and system features

Notification permission is requested only if you enable reminders. Android delivers reminders and home-screen widgets. File access is opened only when you choose a backup to restore or select a destination through the system picker.

Exports and sharing

Backup, CSV, Excel, and PDF files are created only on your request. Sharing opens Android's share sheet; only the app or person you select receives that file. Restoring a JSON backup replaces current counter data only after confirmation.

Google Play purchases

If you buy Pro, Google Play processes the payment under Google's policies. Ultimate Counter receives product and entitlement status needed to unlock Pro; it does not receive your full card or bank details. Purchase restoration is requested only when you choose it.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear them or uninstall the app. Use Clear all data in Settings to remove counters and history. Exported files remain wherever you placed them until you delete them there. Android or device backups may follow your operating-system settings.

No sale or advertising use

Ultimate Counter does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, share it for behavioral profiling, or collect persistent device identifiers.

Policy changes

If data practices materially change, this policy and its effective date will be updated with the app release. The Play Store Data safety disclosure should always match this policy and the shipped app.

Developer and privacy contact

Ultimate Counter is the app covered by this policy. For privacy requests or questions, use the developer contact email on the official Google Play listing.

Private by design. Shared by choice.
