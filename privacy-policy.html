TALLYPULSE · IndieFocus Labs

Privacy policy

Effective October 8, 2026

At a glance

TallyPulse is published by IndieFocus Labs. It needs no account, shows no ads, and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. Counter records are processed on your device and are not automatically sent to the developer. You control any export or sharing.

Information on your device

The app stores counter names, icons, colours, values, timestamped entries, entry notes, targets, categories, reset rules, reminder schedules, ordering, appearance preferences, guide progress, widget selections, and local Pro/trial status to provide its features. Counters you create may contain health, financial, or other personal information; this information stays local unless you choose to share it. Learning guides use temporary example counters, which are removed when the lesson closes. Counters you explicitly create or add from templates are retained.

Storage and device backup

Records are held in Android’s private app storage. Home-screen widgets use app-owned preferences and display the counters you select to anyone who can view your home screen. Notifications may display counter names on the lock screen; visibility depends on your Android settings. There is no developer-hosted sync or cloud backup. This release disables Android automatic app backup and excludes records and preferences from Android cloud backup and device transfer rules. Device manufacturers may implement transfer differently, so use a JSON export when moving your data. Local storage relies on Android’s protections; exported files are not encrypted by TallyPulse.

Permissions and reminders

TallyPulse requests notification permission when you enable reminders. You can disable individual reminders or revoke permission in Android Settings. Android schedules reminders locally and uses the boot receiver to restore schedules after a restart. The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier access. The system file picker opens only when you choose a backup file or a destination for saving it.

Exports and sharing

JSON backup, CSV, Excel, and PDF files are generated only when you choose to export. They can include counter records, notes, and history. Android’s file picker and share sheet let you choose a destination or receiving app; that destination handles the file under its own policies. Sharing may leave temporary copies in the app’s private cache. A JSON restore replaces current counters and history only after confirmation; it does not restore appearance settings or grant a paid entitlement.

Google Play and Pro

Google Play handles purchases under its own privacy and payment policies. The app connects to Play to retrieve product availability and prices and receives purchase identifiers, verification data, and purchase status to unlock Pro and complete transactions. Pro status is stored locally. You can request Restore purchases using the Google Play account that purchased Pro. TallyPulse does not receive your full payment card or bank details and does not send counter records to Google Play for billing. Google Play and Android may separately process installation, device, payment, or crash information under their own policies; the developer may see store statistics and platform crash reports.

Retention and deletion

Counter records remain until you delete them, restore another backup, choose Settings > Clear all data, or uninstall TallyPulse. Clear all data removes counters, history, cached totals, counter reminder schedules, widget counter data, and app-owned export staging copies. Appearance settings, guide completion, and purchase/trial state remain so you can continue using the app. Files saved or shared outside the app remain until you delete them from those destinations. Uninstalling removes private app storage; it does not delete external exports or Google Play’s purchase records.

Support and your choices

Contact IndieFocus Labs at ruprety001@gmail.com for privacy or support questions. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use it to answer your request, retaining it only as needed for support and any applicable legal obligations. Do not attach sensitive counter records unless you intend to share them. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase local counters; use the app’s deletion controls. TallyPulse does not sell personal data or use counter records for advertising or profiling.

Policy changes

Material changes to these data practices will be reflected in this policy and its effective date. The in-app policy, public policy, and Play Store Data safety disclosure must describe the released app consistently.
